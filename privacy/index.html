Pandeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Short version: Pandeck does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Pandeck saves a few things on your own device so the app remembers them: your settings (game, number of bots, bot speed, sound), your game stats (wins, streaks, best time), the name and animal you pick for playing with friends, and a random ID that the app makes for your device. This never leaves your phone unless you play with friends (below). Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Playing with friends

When you create or join a lobby, the app sends your chosen name, your animal, the random device ID and your game moves to the other players in that lobby, so everyone sees the same game. These live messages pass through Supabase (a cloud service) and are not stored by Pandeck. Lobbies end when the host leaves or after 30 minutes with nothing happening. Only share a lobby code with people you want to play with.

What Pandeck does not do

  • No sign-in or account, and no email, phone number or contacts.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

Pandeck is a family-friendly game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Names typed for friend games should be nicknames, not real full names.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
